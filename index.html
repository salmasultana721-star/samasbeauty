🌐 Update Prices for All Clients
Because this app is a static file, price changes save on your device only. To push prices to all clients:

1. Edit prices in the Menu tab above
2. Tap Download Updated App below
3. Go to netlify.app → Sites → your site → Deploys
4. Drag & drop the downloaded index.html onto the deploy drop-zone
5. All clients instantly see the new prices ✓
📋 Client Directory (0)
Every client who books is automatically saved here. Send a WhatsApp broadcast to all contacts at once, or search for individuals.
📣 Broadcast Message
Type your offer or announcement below. Tap Send to All to open WhatsApp for each contact — or copy the full list to paste into a WhatsApp group.
💡 Tip: WhatsApp doesn't support true bulk messaging from a personal number. Use WhatsApp Business broadcast lists (up to 256 contacts) or copy all numbers and paste into a WhatsApp group you create for clients.
📱 WhatsApp API Setup
Enter your Meta WhatsApp Business API credentials to enable booking confirmations and 30-minute reminders. Get these from developers.facebook.com → WhatsApp → API Setup.
⚠️ 30-min reminders fire when the app is open in a browser tab. For guaranteed delivery (even when app is closed), connect to a free automation tool like n8n or Make.com to poll and send reminders server-side.
💳 Payment Collection
Recommended: Stripe Payment Links (Free to Set Up)
The easiest and free way to collect payments is via Stripe Payment Links. Stripe charges 1.5% + 20p per transaction (UK cards) — no monthly fees.
Step 1 — Create Free Stripe Account
Go to stripe.com → sign up free → no monthly cost
Step 2 — Create Payment Links
Dashboard → Payment Links → Create link per service (e.g. "Eyebrow Threading — £3.50")
Step 3 — Send After Booking
Add a Stripe payment link to your booking confirmation WhatsApp message. Clients pay via card on any device.
🔒 How Secure Is It?
Stripe is PCI-DSS Level 1 certified — the highest level of payment security. Card details are never handled by this app; clients enter them directly on Stripe's encrypted servers. Your Stripe dashboard shows all payments instantly.
💰 Other Free Options
• PayPal.me — share your PayPal link in WhatsApp confirmation
• SumUp — free card reader for in-person payments (1.69% per swipe)
• Square — free POS for walk-ins + online payment links
Built-in card processing requires a backend server. For now, use Stripe Payment Links — it's the simplest, safest, zero-monthly-cost option for a salon your size.

Privacy Policy
Beauty Zone  ·  Last updated May 2026
🏪 Who We Are
Beauty Zone. We operate this booking app for clients to book beauty treatments. Contact us at the salon for any privacy queries.
📋 What Data We Collect
We collect your full name and phone number when you make a booking. We do not collect email addresses, payment data, IP addresses, or any sensitive personal data.
🎯 Why We Collect It
Your details are used solely to: (1) Identify your booking and reference, (2) Send appointment reminders via WhatsApp, (3) Allow you to manage bookings using your phone number as a lookup.
🔒 How We Store It
Booking data is stored locally in your browser (localStorage). No tracking cookies, no advertising cookies, and no third-party analytics tools are used. If a cloud database is enabled, data is stored securely with encryption at rest.
🚫 Who We Share It With
We do not sell, share, rent, or transfer your personal data to any third parties. WhatsApp reminders are sent only using the number you provide at booking.
⏰ How Long We Keep It
Booking records are kept for 12 months after your last appointment. You may request deletion at any time using the button below or by contacting us directly.
✅ Your Rights (UK GDPR)
Under UK GDPR you have the right to: Access your data  ·  Rectify inaccurate data  ·  Erase your data  ·  Restrict processing  ·  Object to processing  ·  Data portability. Contact us at the salon to exercise any of these rights.
🍪 Cookies & Storage
This app uses only functional browser localStorage — not tracking cookies. We store: your booking reference, phone number for lookup, and your consent preference. Nothing else.
🗑️ Deleting Your Data
Tap the button below to instantly delete all locally stored data from your device. To remove data from our database, contact us directly and we will process your request within 30 days (as required by UK GDPR).
📬 Complaints
For privacy concerns contact Beauty Zone directly. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the ICO (Information Commissioner's Office) at ico.org.uk or call 0303 123 1113.
